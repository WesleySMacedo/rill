# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Rill community is committed to providing a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view. We value technical rigor, curiosity, clear disagreement, and collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions, reviews, and other community spaces.
- Critique ideas and implementation details without attacking people.
- Explain technical disagreement with evidence and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect different perspectives and experience levels.
- Keep feedback relevant to the project and its safety model.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and work to learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language or conduct.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This Code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing or participating in the Rill project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this Code privately to the project maintainer using the contact channel published on the [Rill website](https://rill.wesleysmacedo.com). Do not use a public issue for a sensitive report. Include relevant links or context, and avoid sharing private information that is not necessary.

The maintainer will review reports, protect the privacy of people involved to the extent practical, and take an action proportionate to the behavior. Possible responses include a request to stop, content removal, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces. The maintainer will communicate the decision to the people directly involved where appropriate.
